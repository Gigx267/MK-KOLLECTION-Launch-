Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

UK orders take 1-2 business days. International orders take 3-7 business days. All orders over £250 ship free via Express.

Are your wigs pre-plucked?

Yes! All of our 13x6 frontal wigs come pre-plucked with a natural hairline and bleached knots for an undetectable melt.

What is your return policy?

For hygiene reasons, we only accept returns on unopened, unworn hair extensions in their original packaging within 14 days of delivery.
